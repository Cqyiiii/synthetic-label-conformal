Purpose and decision record

The release makes the authors' research artifact available as synthetic-conformal, with a common launcher, the eight frozen tabular inputs, numerical HelpSteer2 caches, designs and reference tables. The authors selected MIT for their original code. Third-party datasets retain their own licenses and attribution. Public documentation and packaging metadata were updated; the scientific source, parameter grids, seed plans, cached inputs and reference results were preserved byte for byte.

The supported entry point is run.py. Earlier baseline, labeling, notebook and evidence-building code remains inspectable, but some historical entry points require their original full-text or directory layout. The earlier Civil Comments screening study is source only and is separate from the main benchmark and HelpSteer evidence.

HelpSteer2 caches and joins

The processed NVIDIA HelpSteer2 input retains 21,352 rows and eleven fields: row ID, five human ratings, original split, prompt-group key, role and two text-length fields. Outer roles contain 18,513 pool rows, 801 base-training rows, 500 pilot rows, 500 conformal rows and 1,038 test rows. The downstream runner divides the conformal role into development and final-calibration subsets. Raw prompt and response text is omitted.

There are 2,839 cached 768-dimensional float32 feature vectors, derived from concatenated normalized prompt and response embeddings from sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2. IDs are unique, finite feature arrays join exactly to the four relevant roles, and the stored role labels agree with the processed table. The two 500-row synthetic-rating files have successful status for every row and exactly match the prediction cache. Their recorded model identities are deepseek-v4-flash and deepseek-v4-pro. These are historical inputs; no new model service was called.

Zero prompt keys span outer roles. This does not eliminate dependence among responses to a prompt or in the original paired/pool subdivision. The CC BY 4.0 source attribution is preserved. No encoder weights, raw provider response envelopes or provider request identifiers are included.

Interpretation boundaries

  • The supplied new coverage selectors do not consistently outperform fixed unit PPI; adverse and null results remain included.
  • Random splits overlap. Paired bootstrap intervals are unadjusted and describe split variation, not independent population sampling.
  • Diamonds retains 146 exact duplicate full records and 345 duplicate X rows; row separation is not entity independence.
  • HelpSteer responses are clustered by prompt. Prompt separation of outer roles does not establish row-iid conformal validity.
  • Historical HelpSteer neural fits and the recorded CPU reconstruction are not numerically identical.
  • Civil Comments is historical source only; its input and label caches are not part of this release.
  • Historical generation/report scripts may require their original full-text layout and optional dependencies. run.py is the supported supplied-cache entry point.

In particular, paired intervals concern variation across overlapping random splits and are not multiple-comparison-adjusted. Finite grouped coverage, Fourier, WSC and ERT diagnostics do not prove pointwise conditional validity. Final HelpSteer calibration contains 400 responses from 200 prompts; the official test subset contains 1,038 responses from 518 prompts. The historical CPU reconstruction differs from archived fits by up to approximately 0.012516 raw pinball, 0.042389 coverage, 0.004600 MSCE, and 30 selected epochs. These inherited boundaries were documented rather than retuned during release preparation.
